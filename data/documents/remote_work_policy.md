---

## 1. Overview & Policy Intent

Enterprise Inc. champions flexibility while ensuring high collaboration, cybersecurity discipline, and corporate cohesion. This policy outlines work models, home office setup allowances, communication norms, and security guidelines for hybrid and remote employees.

---

## 2. Workplace Classification Models

Every position at Enterprise Inc. is mapped to one of three operational work models:

### 2.1 Hybrid Model (Default Corporate Standard)
- **Anchor Days:** Employees work in their designated regional corporate office a minimum of **two (2) days per week** (Company-wide anchor days are **Tuesday** and **Thursday**).
- **Flexible Days:** Monday, Wednesday, and Friday may be worked from an approved home location.
- **Team Alignments:** Department leads may schedule specific team on-sites or sprint planning days with two weeks advance notification.

### 2.2 Fully Remote Model
- **Eligibility:** Available for roles designated as "Location Agnostic" or for individuals residing greater than **50 miles (80 km)** from the nearest corporate office.
- **Approval:** Requires formal justification from the Director, approval by the Business Unit VP, and sign-off from People Operations.
- **Quarterly On-Sites:** Fully remote staff are expected to travel to headquarters for scheduled quarterly team planning summits (travel and lodging covered by company T&E).

### 2.3 Office-Centric Model
- Roles requiring specialized physical lab equipment, hardware inventory management, executive physical security, or reception duties operate 5 days on-site.

## 3. Financial Subsidies & Equipment Allowances

### 3.1 One-Time Home Office Ergonomic Stipend
- Eligible hybrid and remote employees may claim up to **$750 USD** (or local currency equivalent) during their first 90 days of employment.
- **Eligible Items:** Ergonomic task chair, motorized sit-stand desk, external monitor, laptop stand, surge protector, keyboard, and mouse.
- **Ineligible Items:** Gaming peripherals, decorative lighting, home furniture not dedicated to work.
- **Reimbursement Procedure:** Incur expenses on personal card, submit receipts via Expensify under category `Home Office Setup (One-Time)`.

### 3.2 Monthly Internet & Connectivity Allowance
- Employees receive a non-taxable monthly connectivity stipend of **$60.00 USD** credited automatically via payroll to offset high-speed broadband utilities.
- No receipts are required for the standard monthly stipend.

## 4. Operational Hours & Collaboration Standards

- **Core Hours:** All staff must be accessible on Slack and email during regional core hours: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local timezone**.
- **Calendar Visibility:** Google Calendars must be shared internally with "Show Details" enabled. Working hours and lunch blocks must be clearly maintained.
- **Video Etiquette:** Camera usage is encouraged for 1:1s, client meetings, and team rituals. Appropriate attire and professional virtual background or blur are mandatory when interacting with clients.
- **Asynchronous Communication:** For non-urgent matters, utilize documentation in Notion/Confluence and Slack threads rather than scheduling ad-hoc meetings.

---

## 5. Work From Anywhere (Temporary Remote Travel)

- **Domestic Travel:** Employees may work from an alternate domestic location for up to **30 calendar days per calendar year** with manager notification.
- **International Remote Work ("Work from Abroad"):**
  - Permitted for up to **15 business days per year** to prevent corporate tax nexus creation and labor compliance liabilities.
  - Must be requested via the HR portal at least **3 weeks prior** to departure.
  - Restricted countries: Due to export control and international sanction laws, corporate assets must not enter embargoed or high-risk ITAR nations under any circumstances.

---

## 6. Physical & Environmental Security

1. **Private Workspace:** When handling Sensitive or Confidential data, employees must work in an enclosed space where non-company members cannot view screens or overhear calls.
2. **Screen Privacy Filters:** Mandatory when working from airports, public cafes, or trains. IT distributes 3M magnetic privacy filters upon request.
3. **Locking Devices:** Devices must be locked (`Win+L` on Windows, `Cmd+Ctrl+Q` on macOS) whenever unattended, even inside home residences.
