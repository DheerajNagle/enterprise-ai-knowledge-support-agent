# Enterprise Travel & Business Expense (T&E) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-2024-006  
**Version:** 5.1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Owner:** Global Corporate Finance & Controller  
**Applicability:** All employees incurring business-related travel, entertainment, and operational expenses  

---

## 1. General Principles & Purpose

Enterprise Inc. reimburses employees for all reasonable, necessary, and authorized business expenses incurred while conducting company business. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment, balance cost with efficiency, and treat company funds with fiduciary responsibility.

---

## 2. Meals & Daily Subsistence Allowances (Per Diem)

When traveling overnight on official company business, employees are entitled to daily meal reimbursements:

### 2.1 Daily Meal Spending Limits
- **Domestic Business Travel:** Up to **$75.00 USD per day**
  - Suggested allocation: Breakfast: $15.00 | Lunch: $25.00 | Dinner: $35.00
- **International Business Travel:** Up to **$100.00 USD per day**
  - Suggested allocation: Breakfast: $20.00 | Lunch: $35.00 | Dinner: $45.00
- **Team & Client Meals:** When dining with clients or hosting an authorized team dinner, the host of highest seniority must pay and itemize all attendee names in the expense report.

### 2.2 Alcohol Policy
- Alcoholic beverages are reimbursable only during business dinners with external clients or authorized team milestone events (capped at two drinks per attendee).
- Alcohol purchased during solitary travel dinners or room-service snacks is non-reimbursable.

---

## 3. Air Travel & Rail Transportation

- **Booking Platform:** All flights must be booked through our corporate travel portal (**Navan / TripActions**).
- **Cabin Class Standards:**
  - **Flights under 6 hours duration:** Standard **Economy Class**.
  - **Non-stop flights exceeding 6 hours duration (Transatlantic / Transpacific):** **Premium Economy** or **Business Class** permitted with prior Vice President authorization.
- **Checked Baggage:** Up to one (1) standard checked bag fee is fully reimbursable per itinerary.
- **In-Flight Wi-Fi:** Reimbursable for flights during normal business hours where active work is performed.

---

## 4. Lodging & Hotel Accommodations

- **Maximum Nightly Room Caps (Excluding Taxes):**
  - **Standard Metros:** Up to **$250.00 USD / night**.
  - **Tier 1 High-Cost Metros (San Francisco, New York, London, Zurich, Tokyo, Singapore):** Up to **$350.00 USD / night**.
- **Alternative Accommodations:** Corporate apartment rentals or Airbnb are acceptable provided total accommodation costs do not exceed local hotel benchmark rates.
- **Incidental Expenses:** Personal laundry services are reimbursable only for business trips exceeding five (5) consecutive business days. In-room movie rentals, spa services, and mini-bar charges are strictly non-reimbursable.

---

## 5. Ground Transportation & Mileage

- **Rideshare Services:** UberX, Lyft Standard, or licensed taxis are preferred for airport and client transfers. Premium tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are non-reimbursable.
- **Rental Cars:** Intermediate or compact sedans booked through corporate Avis/Hertz agreements. Decline optional rental agency CDW/LDW insurance domestically as corporate credit cards provide primary coverage.
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage:** Reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate (**$0.67 per mile** for 2024). Commute from residence to primary office is not eligible for mileage reimbursement.

---

## 6. Expense Submission & Approval Thresholds

1. **Submission Portal:** Submit all expense reports via **Expensify** (`https://expensify.enterprise.internal`).
2. **Timeline:** Reports must be submitted within **thirty (30) calendar days** of the expense date. Expenses submitted beyond 60 days require Finance Controller review and may be rejected.
3. **Receipt Requirement:** Itemized receipts are mandatory for any single transaction equal to or exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card authorization slips lacking itemized line items are insufficient.
4. **Approval Matrix:**
   - **$0.01 – $500.00:** Direct Manager Approval
   - **$500.01 – $2,500.00:** Department Director Approval
   - **$2,500.01 and above:** Business Unit Vice President & Finance Controller Approval
5. **Reimbursement SLA:** Approved expense reports are processed via direct deposit within **five (5) to seven (7) business days**.
