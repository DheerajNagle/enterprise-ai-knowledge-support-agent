## 1. Purpose & Core Principles

Enterprise Inc. recognizes the importance of personal well-being, work-life integration, and family responsibilities. This policy sets forth the guidelines and procedures governing all statutory and company-sponsored leave categories. All leaves must be planned in consultation with the direct manager to ensure operational continuity while supporting employee rest and rejuvenation.

---

## 2. Leave Categories and Entitlements

### 2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO) / Annual Vacation
- **Annual Accrual:** Full-time employees accrue **24 business days** of paid vacation per calendar year (accrued at the rate of **2.0 days per completed month of service**).
- **Pro-Rating:** New hires starting mid-year accrue vacation on a pro-rata basis beginning on their official start date.
- **Carryover & Rollover Cap:** A maximum of **5 unused vacation days** may be rolled over into the following calendar year. Rolled-over days must be utilized before **March 31** of the new year, after which they expire without financial compensation unless prohibited by local labor regulations.
- **Negative Balances:** Employees may advance up to 3 days of unearned PTO with prior written approval from their Department Director.

### 2.2 Sick & Medical Leave
- **Annual Entitlement:** Employees receive **10 paid sick days** on January 1 of each calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue monthly and does not roll over.
- **Notification Requirement:** In the event of unforeseen illness, employees must notify their manager and log the absence in the Workday portal before **09:30 AM local time** on the first day of absence.
- **Medical Certification:** A signed medical certificate from a licensed healthcare professional is mandatory for sick leaves exceeding **three (3) consecutive working days**.
- **Extended Medical Absence:** Absences exceeding 10 consecutive days transition to Short-Term Disability (STD) managed through our insurance carrier.

### 2.3 Parental & Family Care Leave
- **Primary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible employees who welcome a child through birth, adoption, or surrogacy receive **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
- **Eligibility Window:** Parental leave must be taken within twelve (12) months following the date of birth or formal placement. Leave may be taken in a single block or split into two blocks with departmental approval.

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave
- **Immediate Family (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling):** Up to **5 consecutive paid business days**.
- **Extended Family (Grandparent, In-Law, Aunt/Uncle):** Up to **3 consecutive paid business days**.
- Additional unpaid leave or vacation days may be requested for international travel requirements.

### 2.5 Public & Floating Holidays
- The enterprise observes **11 standard public holidays** published on the internal intranet calendar in December.
- Employees receive **2 Floating Holidays** per calendar year to observe cultural, religious, or personal days of significance. Floating holidays do not roll over.

### 2.6 Sabbatical Leave
Employees with **three (3) or more years of continuous service** may apply for an unpaid sabbatical ranging from **30 to 90 calendar days** for professional development, education, or personal enrichment. Approval requires endorsement by the Department Head and the Chief People Officer.

---

## 3. Application, Approvals & Workflow

1. **Submission Portal:** All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR Portal (`https://workday.enterprise.internal`).
2. **Advance Notice:**
   - Single-day vacation: At least **2 business days** prior notice.
   - Vacations of 3–5 days: At least **10 business days** prior notice.
   - Vacations exceeding 5 consecutive days: At least **20 business days** prior notice.
3. **Manager SLA:** People managers must approve or reject leave requests within **48 hours** of submission. Rejections must provide documented operational justification.
4. **Blackout Periods:** Critical business release cycles (such as Q4 Financial Close or major platform migrations) may designate blackout windows during which discretionary PTO requires VP authorization.

---

## 4. Separation & Encashment

- Upon separation of employment (resignation or termination), accrued and unused standard PTO will be encashed in the final payroll cycle at the employee's base salary rate.
- Sick leave, floating holidays, and parental leave balances are non-encashable upon separation.
