## 1. Welcome to Enterprise Inc.!

Welcome to the team! Our onboarding program is designed to integrate you smoothly into our culture, equip you with the right tools, and provide clear milestones for your first month.

---

## 2. Day 1: Identity, IT Hardware & Core Setup

### 2.1 Identity Verification & HR Compliance
- Complete the federal I-9 form (or local jurisdictional right-to-work verification) by 12:00 PM on your first day.
- Upload supporting identification documents (e.g., Passport, Driver's License) to the Workday onboarding task list.

### 2.2 Corporate Identity & Account Activation
1. Navigate to the enterprise single sign-on portal: `https://identity.enterprise.internal`.
2. Enter your corporate email (`firstname.lastname@enterprise.internal`) and the temporary setup passphrase sent by HR.
3. Enroll your mobile device in **Okta Verify** with biometric number matching enabled.
4. Set up your permanent 16-character corporate password following password policy guidelines.

### 2.3 Essential Collaboration Channels (Slack)
Join the following mandatory and community Slack channels:
- `#announcements`: Company-wide executive updates.
- `#general`: Daily company chatter and team highlights.
- `#help-it`: Real-time IT support and system outage notifications.
- `#help-people-ops`: HR benefits, payroll, and PTO questions.
- `#new-hires-2024`: Your onboarding cohort peer discussion group.

### 2.4 Meet Your Onboarding Buddy
Every new hire is paired with a designated team buddy outside their immediate managerial chain. Your buddy will reach out via Slack on Day 1 to schedule a virtual coffee and answer informal organizational questions.

## 3. Week 1: Payroll, Benefits & Mandatory Training

### 3.1 Direct Deposit & Tax Withholdings
Log in to **Workday** (`https://workday.enterprise.internal`) under `Pay & Benefits`:
- Add bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit payroll.
- Complete W-4 (or local tax withholding declarations).

### 3.2 Benefits Enrollment (30-Day Window)
- You have **thirty (30) calendar days** from your hire date to elect health insurance (Medical, Dental, Vision, HSA/FSA) and 401(k) / pension matching.
- Coverage takes effect on the **first day of the calendar month following your hire date**.
- If no election is made within 30 days, default core employee-only coverage is automatically assigned until the next annual Open Enrollment period in November.

### 3.3 Mandatory Compliance Courses
The following courses must be completed in the **Enterprise Learning Management Portal (LMS)** within **fourteen (14) days** of hire:
1. *Cybersecurity Awareness & Phishing Defense (45 mins)*
2. *Preventing Workplace Harassment & Fostering Inclusion (60 mins)*
3. *Data Privacy & GDPR/CCPA Compliance (30 mins)*
4. *Code of Business Ethics & Insider Trading (30 mins)*

## 4. Weeks 2 to 4: Technical Environment & Team Integration

### 4.1 Engineering & Developer Access Provisioning
For software engineers, data scientists, and technical product managers:
- **GitLab Enterprise:** Request project group access via IT Self-Service portal.
- **Cloud Sandboxes:** Request AWS/GCP non-production sandbox tenant access through ticket category `Access/Identity` -> `Developer Cloud Access`.
- **Docker & Local Setup:** Follow department-specific setup wikis on Confluence at `https://wiki.enterprise.internal/engineering/onboarding`.

### 4.2 30-60-90 Day Goal Setting
Schedule structured alignment check-ins with your manager:
- **Day 30 Milestone:** Complete all training, ship first small feature/task, understand primary stakeholder workflows.
- **Day 60 Milestone:** Independently handle assigned project tracks, participate in sprint retrospectives.
- **Day 90 Milestone:** Full autonomy on standard work items, establish formal quarterly OKRs.
